첫 커스텀 MAME 빌드와 EKMame 학습

EKMame을 교과서 삼아 MAME를 공부하다

기존 Kaillera와 MAME 환경이 기준에 미치지 못한다는 결론에 이르렀고, EKMame을 분석하며 메탈슬러그 시리즈 롬핵(ROM Hack)을 사용할 수 있는 Kaillera환경을 구축하려던 커스텀 빌드였으나,

ASM 코어 에뮬레이션의 카일레라 미지원, 또한 불안정한 Kaillera 환경과 부족한 부가기능의 한계를 확인합니다.

First Custom MAME Build & EKMame Study

Studying MAME using EKMame as a textbook

I concluded that the existing Kaillera and MAME environments did not meet the requirements,

and although this was a custom build intended to create a Kaillera environment capable of using Metal Slug series ROM hacks by analyzing EKMame,

I found that ASM core emulation does not support Kaillera, and that the environment is unstable with limited additional features.

PORTING · 2019.10

Final Burn Alpha Legacy Port

03 / 10 · 2019.10

Final Burn Alpha(FBA) 포팅 시도

롬핵 리셋 문제를 해결하기 위해 FBA를 선택, 그러나....

커스텀 MAME 테스트 중 일부 롬핵에서 리셋 문제가 발생하는 것을 확인하고, Final Burn Alpha로 방향을 전환했습니다.

MAME 분석 경험을 바탕으로 구버전 FBA에 직접 코드를 이식, 그러나 Kailera와 전혀 맞지 않는 환경으로 오프라인에서만 사용가능하다는 한계가 있었습니다.

Porting Drivers to Final Burn Alpha (FBA)

Pivoting to FBA's lightweight architecture to resolve ROM hack resets, However....

After confirming that some custom MAME tests were experiencing reset issues with certain ROM kernels,

I have shifted our focus to Final Burn Alpha. Based on my experience with MAME analysis,

I ported the code to an older version of FBA, but it had the limitation of being usable only offline and not compatible at all with Kailera.

The result exceeded expectations, but there were limitations with unsupported drivers and a missing ROMSET; however,

this laid the groundwork for building a compilation environment for Fightcade's emulator source code in the future

proposed to the development team to port the overclocking option to the released FC2 FBNeo source,

but due to conflicts with the existing infrastructure architecture (replay and matchmaking lobbies), only the Metal Slug Hack Rom driver was implemented,

and the overclocking feature was rejected. This REDGGPO Also too.
